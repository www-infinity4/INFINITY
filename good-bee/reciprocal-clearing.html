The missing reciprocating system

A specific store promise becomes a general Infinity unit only after the original claim is retired.

Government, nonprofit, employer, insurer, or other institutional funding can purchase defined destinations, products, housing, health support, transportation, food, tools, and services. New Hope or Infinity Health allocates those store-card claims. A person or business may later exchange an eligible claim for general Infinity units, but the source claim is permanently locked and cancelled first.

Reciprocal clearing loop

Funding enters once. Value can circulate many times without being counted twice.

The system records every stage separately so AI can automate allocation, verification, exchange, taxes, receipts, and profit sharing without inventing unsupported value.

01

Program purchases capacity

A government agency or another funder contracts with Infinity Health, New Hope, Good Bee, or an approved provider for defined goods and services.

Example: accessible lodging nights, meals, rides, home setup, health navigation, tools, seed, or verified work.
02

Store claims enter a program pool

The funded inventory is represented as store-card claims with exact restrictions, quantities, expiration, providers, and refund terms.

The claim is not yet a general Infinity unit.
03

AI allocates within policy

New Hope or Infinity Health AI matches claims to eligible people, pay packages, destinations, support plans, or business needs.

Human appeal and correction remain available.
04

Person chooses use or exchange

The holder may use the promised product or service, keep the claim, reject it, or request conversion when program rules allow.

No automatic surrender of a benefit without the person’s approval.
05

Clearinghouse verifies the claim

Infinity confirms funding, ownership, restrictions, expiration, taxes, fraud checks, merchant status, and whether conversion is legally permitted.

Unavailable, disputed, or restricted claims cannot pass clearing.
06

Original card is locked and retired

The exact claim is marked converted, receives a retirement receipt, and can no longer buy the original product or service.

This is the anti-double-spend point.
07

Infinity units are issued

The settlement reserve issues the published exchange amount to the user’s Infinity Wallet only after retirement completes.

The issuance references the retired claim and cannot exceed approved value.
08

Units circulate through real work

People spend units on approved goods and services. Businesses, workers, creators, growers, builders, researchers, and caregivers earn them.

Capacity must be verified before product-backed claims are issued.
09

Infinity buys and recirculates value

Infinity may purchase eligible units or settlement claims through a published treasury policy, then direct verified surplus into operating, reserve, and community pools.

No hidden buyback rate or guaranteed investment return.

Four-ledger architecture

Never mix funding, promises, circulating units, and profit.

A

Program Funding Ledger

Records money or contractual funding actually received from government agencies, grants, employers, insurers, donations, customers, or other approved sources.

  • Pledged
  • Awarded
  • Received
  • Restricted
  • Committed
  • Spent
  • Refunded
B

Store Claim Ledger

Records the specific product, service, destination, inventory, support, or merchant value represented by every store card.

  • Issued
  • Allocated
  • Used
  • Exchanged
  • Expired
  • Cancelled
  • Retired
C

Infinity Unit Ledger

Records general transferable units after valid issuance, including ownership, payments, earnings, holds, disputes, taxes, and retirement.

  • Available
  • Held
  • Transferred
  • Earned
  • Spent
  • Disputed
  • Retired
D

Treasury and Profit Pool

Records audited reserves, operating revenue, verified surplus, public-benefit allocations, business investment, losses, and distributions.

  • Settlement reserve
  • Operations
  • Community pool
  • Business seed pool
  • Loss reserve
  • Profit distribution

Basic participation allowance

Up to 100 Infinity units per day as a funded safety ceiling—not an unsupported mint.

The AI does not wait for a person with no current product or service to fail. When a designated public-benefit or community pool has available funding, the wallet may authorize up to 100 units per day for approved necessities such as food, seed, tools, transportation, communication, hygiene, and steps toward producing a good or service.

Funded firstThe allowance draws from received and designated resources, not imaginary revenue.
Daily ceiling“100” is a maximum authorization, not a requirement to spend or an investment promise.

AI work and business engine

The wallet looks for useful work before declaring someone unproductive.

Interest becomes a role

A person who watches many movies could index stories, verify cast and release details, create accessibility notes, write structured summaries, compare editions, flag errors, or curate themed channels.

Conversation becomes documented work

People who develop Infinity through serious planning with AI can receive attributed project records, task receipts, contribution history, and policy-defined compensation after human-reviewable verification.

Capacity controls issuance

A barber’s available appointments, a grower’s eggs, a builder’s units, a designer’s deliverables, or a caregiver’s hours must be verified before product or service claims are issued.

AI handles the hard administration

The system can organize invoices, taxes, receipts, inventory, schedules, contracts, customer communication, and compliance tasks while showing the human what was done.

Seed businesses

The daily allowance and business pool can provide food seed, materials, tools, software access, transportation, training, or a first customer rather than only distributing passive support.

Profit returns through rules

Verified network surplus can support reserves, public services, business expansion, contributor compensation, and broad distributions according to a published formula that no single AI can secretly change.

Rules that make the loop real

Infinity can aim to become a reliable transfer system, but strength comes from verification—not a claim that it is automatically stronger than the dollar.
