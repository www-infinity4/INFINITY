Infinity Reciprocal Clearing Loop

Convert an eligible store claim without leaving two spendable copies.

A store card represents a specific product, service, destination, merchant group, or benefit. When conversion is permitted, Infinity verifies the funding and ownership, locks the source claim, permanently retires it, and only then issues the approved general Infinity units.

Earlier Mario Shop World mechanics, preserved as Shop World

Learn to inspect a deal before spending anything.

The earlier system treated auctions like levels: grab an item, mint a demonstration token, charge the bid capacitor, accept or reject the offer, and build combinations. This page keeps those mechanics as consumer education and a front-end demonstration.

  • Grab: adds the selected offer to the demonstration cart and mints a named demo token.
  • Accept: records the offer as accepted and adds 5 demo coins.
  • Reject: records the offer as rejected and adds 3 demo coins for identifying a bad deal.
  • Bid: requires at least 0.2 capacitor charge and consumes 0.3 charge.
  • Combos: Collector Flip, Tech Combo, Triple World Grind, and Retro Chain remain named achievements.

The physical object and the digital record stay together
