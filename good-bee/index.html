Good Bee Honey Coin

A physical NFC key to real goods and services

The coin does not need to carry private records or a spendable balance. Its NFC tag opens a secure Infinity record that shows the person’s approved package and remaining benefit units.

Tap coin or card→Verify rotating credential→Show approved benefit→Confirm with the person→Record fulfillment
  • No medical history, identity documents, bank keys, or secret balance stored on the NFC tag.
  • A lost coin can be frozen and replaced without losing the underlying package.
  • High-value actions require an additional approval—not a tap alone.
  • Every fulfillment creates a receipt visible to the person and authorized coordinator.

Support that respects independence

Help when needed, privacy when not

Chosen support circle

The resident selects family, friends, nurses, attendants, coordinators, or advocates who may be contacted and what each person can see or do.

Accessible communication

Voice calling, messaging, switch access, eye-gaze access, scheduled check-ins, and emergency calling can be configured around the resident.

Care handoff

A move plan verifies that prescriptions, equipment, benefits, physicians, home-health providers, and emergency contacts continue across the transition.

Human decisions

AI may organize choices and detect missing steps, but it does not decide whether a person must move, receive care, or lose a benefit.

No hidden package, hidden fee, or silent change
