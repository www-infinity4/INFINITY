Global business registry

Scan broadly, verify narrowly.

No single database contains every current business in every country. Coin Planner builds coverage from multiple permitted sources and requires stronger verification before a business can receive money or enter a package.

01

Country and locality index

Use country and subdivision codes, currencies, languages, time zones, tax regions, and destination boundaries.

02

Discover businesses

Query official registries, licensed place services, open map data, destination feeds, and merchant-direct applications.

03

Normalize and deduplicate

Resolve names, aliases, branches, chains, addresses, moved businesses, duplicate place IDs, and conflicting records.

04

Verify control

Confirm that the applicant controls the business, payout account, inventory, prices, contracts, and required licenses.

05

Measure package capacity

Record rooms, meals, rides, hours, products, accessibility, dates, minimum volume, cancellations, delivery, and service limits.

06

Continuously correct

AI, merchants, customers, local contributors, receipts, and public records update status without erasing prior history.

Destination staging

Move the right inventory—not random inventory.

Demand signal

Aggregate destination interest, travel dates, length of stay, accessibility needs, cancellations, weather season, events, and historical package use without exposing personal identities.

Local capacity first

Reserve rooms, meals, transport, experiences, support, rentals, and supplies already located near the destination.

Durable product staging

Move only predictable nonperishable products such as accessible equipment, luggage support, mobility accessories, basic clothing, tools, or package materials.

Perishable and personal items

Purchase food, medicine, fitted equipment, and sensitive personal goods closer to actual use and only with proper consent and rules.

Reverse logistics

Plan unused inventory, returns, repairs, redistribution, donations, resale, recycling, and warranty handling before stock moves.

Local economic share

Show what percentage of package spending remains with destination businesses, workers, producers, and community programs.

Protection and fairness

Cheap packages cannot depend on hidden losses or fake retail prices.

AI can search, negotiate, forecast, bundle, and reconcile, but the package must remain understandable to the traveler, merchant, funder, and auditor.

Benchmark evidenceRetail-equivalent claims include dates, comparable public offers, inclusions, exclusions, and source.
Contract evidenceEvery discount comes from a signed merchant offer, bulk rule, off-peak rate, promotion, or funding agreement.
Merchant consentNo business is enrolled, priced, or paid through scraped information alone.
Funding-gap lockThe package remains planning-only until user contribution plus all verified funding equals or exceeds contracted cost.
No below-cost coercionAI cannot pressure small businesses into unsustainable pricing or conceal service-worker compensation.
Accessibility truthAccessibility claims require structured details and user feedback, not a generic “accessible” label.
Currency and tax clarityLocal currency, exchange basis, taxes, fees, and Infinity settlement terms remain visible.
Package exitCancellation, substitution, refund, failure, emergency, and return-home rules exist before purchase.
