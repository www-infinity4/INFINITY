# Good Bee · Infinity Living

Good Bee is a development prototype for voluntary accessible travel, trial stays, relocation, support coordination, modular housing, NFC benefit credentials, AI-assisted Product Token creation, Store Cards, reciprocal clearing, AI wallet orchestration, and local-first package planning.

## Websites

### Infinity System Map

Open [`infinity-system.html`](infinity-system.html).

This is the master searchable map of the connected creation, funding, housing, travel, business, work, card, wallet, clearing, metal, and fulfillment systems.

### Infinity AI Token Studio

Open [`token-studio.html`](token-studio.html).

The Token Studio gives every convertible object the same subtle upper-right Star mark:

- 15 × 15 pixels;
- same position and hover/focus behavior;
- partial opacity until needed;
- opens the selected object in the creation interface;
- means create, edit, version, convert, connect, or inspect ancestry;
- does not mean favorite, endorsement, verification, ownership, funding, or guaranteed value.

The page:

- ranks the products Infinity needs most;
- scores system demand, scarcity, human usefulness, production readiness, and local capacity;
- identifies the weakest production factor;
- defines the exact Product Token;
- calculates planned supply plus a contingency reserve;
- calculates Avatar Coin requirements for operating identities;
- draws need-ranking, factor, and supply charts;
- saves named Star Blueprint child versions without erasing parents;
- keeps a local prototype version and event ledger;
- links Alien Radio as the first independent working example.

### Infinity Coin Planner

Open [`coin-planner.html`](coin-planner.html).

Coin Planner:

- consumes Product Token needs created by the Token Studio;
- discovers businesses from permitted official, licensed, open, merchant-direct, industry, and community sources;
- organizes countries, localities, destinations, currencies, languages, and business categories;
- deduplicates and verifies listings before merchant enrollment;
- sources hotels, meals, rides, attractions, products, and services locally first;
- requests bulk, off-peak, and direct contracts;
- forecasts likely destination demand;
- stages suitable durable products near likely destinations;
- assembles separate Store Claims into one package;
- distinguishes public retail-equivalent benchmark from actual contracted cost;
- combines traveler, sponsor, government/nonprofit, merchant, and Infinity-pool funding;
- blocks publication while any funding gap remains;
- records fulfillment and settlement separately for every provider.

The demonstration tests how a $1,000 retail-equivalent vacation package could cost the traveler 100 Infinity units only when real discounts and disclosed funding cover the actual contract cost.

## Operating rules

1. Participation and destination choice are voluntary.
2. No destination is assigned by gender, disability, diagnosis, or income.
3. The universal Star mark means creation and conversion access, not favorite, endorsement, verification, ownership, or guaranteed value.
4. A Star change creates a child version and preserves the parent.
5. A Product Token remains a planning or capacity record until provider, capacity, funding, fulfillment, refund, dispute, and rights evidence are verified.
6. A production Avatar Coin cannot be created by creator self-use, bots, hidden or idle pages, or clearly labeled demonstration time.
7. One Avatar Coin locks to one active identity when that identity type requires it.
8. A Store Claim and replacement Infinity units cannot remain spendable at the same time.
9. A package cannot publish while its actual contracted cost has an unfunded gap.
10. A public or licensed discovery listing does not enroll a business; merchant authority and payout must be verified.
11. AI can discover, compare, score, plan, reconcile, and explain but cannot secretly redirect money, force a move, fabricate capacity or use, enroll a business, invent funding, or approve its own transaction.
12. Health, identity, activity, ownership, financial, support, travel, and merchant payout information stays outside public GitHub repositories.
13. Every Star version, claim, package, discount, transfer, fulfillment, retirement, and correction receives a visible audit event.

## Backend required for real operation

Real operation requires protected services and qualified partners for:

- verified creators, contributors, organizations, and usage identities;
- consent and support-circle permissions;
- private accessibility and health information;
- signed Star Blueprint and ancestry manifests;
- Product Token and capacity verification;
- privacy-preserving bot-resistant qualified-use evidence;
- Avatar Coin issuance and identity locking;
- merchant/provider verification;
- live inventory and reservations;
- contracts and signatures;
- payments, prepaid value, holds, settlement, refunds, and disputes;
- Store Claim and Infinity-unit ledgers;
- NFC issuance, freeze, and replacement;
- real bids, auctions, ownership, provenance, shipping, and custody;
- destination forecasting and inventory staging;
- hotel, travel, construction, healthcare, metals, tax, government-benefit, intellectual-property, and consumer-protection compliance;
- security monitoring, backups, audits, and incident response.
