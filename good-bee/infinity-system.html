Full operating flow

From a real business or public program to a person’s useful package.

01

Funding and capacity enter

Government, nonprofits, businesses, customers, sponsors, advertisers, donors, hotels, producers, workers, and service providers supply real funding or verified capacity.

02

Coin Planner builds the registry

Businesses are discovered, normalized, deduplicated, verified, contracted, and organized by country, locality, destination, category, accessibility, price, and capacity.

03

Local and bulk offers are negotiated

Hotels, meals, rides, products, attractions, support, housing, and services are sourced locally first and purchased in bulk when the contract is better.

04

Store Claims are issued

Each specific night, meal, ride, product, support hour, home component, discount, or experience becomes a typed Store Claim with its own provider and rules.

05

AI builds the package

The wallet combines claims into one vacation, move, housing plan, business seed package, daily support plan, or purchase while preserving every underlying record.

06

The person approves

The user approves destination, provider, price, funding, support, conversion, personal-data access, and irreversible actions.

07

Claims are used or cleared

A claim may be fulfilled directly or converted. Conversion requires verification, a quote, locking, permanent retirement, and linked Infinity issuance.

08

Providers settle

Hotels, merchants, workers, caregivers, builders, creators, and other providers receive settlement after the promised result is verified.

09

Work and business grow

AI finds useful roles, documents accepted contributions, helps with taxes and records, and turns ideas into business-plan coins, storefronts, products, and child projects.

10

Value recirculates

Audited surplus can support reserves, New Hope, Infinity Health, basic participation, housing, business tools, contributors, products, and future packages under published governance.

System protections

AI expands the tree, but it does not get unlimited authority.

The point of the architecture is to let Infinity grow without losing the origin, rights, funding, restrictions, contributors, or evidence attached to each branch.
