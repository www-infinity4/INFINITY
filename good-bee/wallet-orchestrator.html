Hotel settlement

100 Infinity units can equal one contracted hotel night.

The price is not universal. It is a published offer: a named hotel, room class, date range, taxes, accessibility features, cancellation terms, and one-night deliverable.

Physical metal access

Digital Infinity is not an unlimited claim on the metal vault.

The wallet keeps general spending units separate from a limited physical-metal access program. A weekly limit prevents a digital balance from instantly draining silver or gold inventory.

WEEKLY SILVER ACCESS 1 approved roll

One configured roll of authenticated silver dimes or private silver dime-sized pieces per person per week, subject to inventory, funding, identity, pickup, and program rules.

A 10-cent denomination is an accounting or face denomination. The actual silver, fabrication, custody, and subsidy costs remain separately recorded.
WEEKLY GOLD ACCESS 1 approved coin

One specifically described gold coin product per person per week, with weight, purity, mint or maker, serial or lot, price basis, and custody record.

“One coin” must never hide its actual metal weight or product cost.

Why the distinction matters

The United States removed silver from circulating dimes in 1965. Modern circulating dimes are copper-nickel, while silver collector versions exist separately. Infinity can design an affordable silver-access program, but it cannot make the physical metal cost disappear by changing the number printed on a coin. The difference must be paid by inventory purchasing, subsidy, donations, production revenue, or another disclosed funding source.

Physical and digital Store Cards

Every verified NFC use can receive at least 50 cents off—while in-person exchange can unlock more.

NFC

Baseline scan discount

A participating merchant can fund a standing $0.50 discount or equivalent Infinity-unit reduction whenever a valid physical card, coin, tag, phone credential, or accessible device is scanned.

IN

In-person bonus

Physical presence can unlock a larger offer because the merchant saves shipping, verifies delivery immediately, builds a customer relationship, or receives a local-program subsidy.

AI

Best-offer selection

The wallet compares the 50-cent baseline, store coupon, loyalty offer, destination package, benefit unit, local bonus, and product-token exchange before presenting the best lawful option.

✓

Receipt and anti-abuse

Each discount has a sponsor, budget, merchant, item, time, device, and redemption receipt so the same offer cannot be duplicated across repeated fake scans.
