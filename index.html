How the pieces connect

A need becomes a verified product and a usable package.

1. Select with ⭐

The same quiet upper-right Star mark opens any convertible project, module, product, service, station, business, offer, chart, component, or work role.

2. Create the Product Token

AI ranks system demand, scarcity, usefulness, readiness, and local capacity; the person saves a named version without erasing its parent.

3. Verify funding and capacity

Programs, customers, sponsors, businesses, workers, hotels, builders, producers, and providers supply real funding or verified capacity.

4. Coin Planner

Finds businesses, verifies control, requests bulk contracts, sources locally, stages appropriate inventory, and calculates actual package cost.

5. Store Claims

Verified Product Tokens become specific hotel nights, meals, rides, products, support hours, station offers, components, discounts, and experiences.

6. AI Wallet

Combines Star versions, Product Tokens, Avatar Coins, claims, and units into understandable actions while preserving every underlying rule.

7. Fulfillment and clearing

Providers fulfill exact claims; when conversion is allowed, the source claim is retired before replacement Infinity units issue.

8. Work and recirculation

Providers and contributors earn settlement; audited surplus can support reserves, New Hope, Infinity Health, housing, tools, businesses, and future products.
