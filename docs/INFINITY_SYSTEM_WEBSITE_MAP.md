# Infinity System Website Map

## Purpose

This document records the connected website files built around the Infinity economic, housing, travel, card, wallet, sourcing, work, and business conversations.

The public system is static-first. Public explanations, calculators, catalogs, package demonstrations, system maps, and non-sensitive schemas live in GitHub. Real accounts, identities, health information, financial data, merchant payouts, government benefits, balances, contracts, inventory, bids, travel records, signing keys, and settlement require protected services outside public repositories.

## Value types

The system does not collapse every asset into one wallet number.

### Program Funding

Actual external resources received through contracts, grants, customers, sponsors, donations, government programs, employers, insurers, or other approved sources.

### Store Claim

A specific economic promise such as one hotel night, one meal, one ride, one product, one support hour, one home component, one discount, or one experience.

### Infinity Unit

A general internal transfer and accounting unit issued only under clearing and treasury policy.

### Metal Access

A separate limited eligibility for a specifically described physical silver or gold product. A general Infinity balance is not an unlimited claim on the physical vault.

### Ownership Record

Evidence covering a card, physical object, relic, digital record, custody, rights, provenance, and transfer history.

### Contribution Record

A reviewable record of accepted human and AI-assisted work, collaborators, rights, approval, and compensation.

### Sponsored Engagement

A funded advertising or merchant-promotion event with a real budget, anti-fraud checks, and measurable visit or conversion.

## Core system rules

1. A specific Store Claim and its replacement Infinity units cannot remain spendable simultaneously.
2. No package is published while actual contracted cost has an unfunded gap.
3. A discovered public business listing does not enroll a merchant or authorize settlement.
4. The person controls destination, support, conversion, work, personal-data access, and irreversible actions.
5. AI may discover, compare, calculate, organize, monitor, and explain but cannot secretly redirect money, invent funding, or approve its own transaction.
6. Physical metal, hotel, product, support, labor, and construction capacity are verified before claims are issued.
7. Private personal and merchant financial information remains outside public GitHub files.
8. Corrections append new events instead of silently erasing or rewriting history.

## User experience rule

The underlying wallet may be as complex as a repository graph. The user-facing interface should answer:

- What can I use now?
- What should I save?
- What fits into a useful package?
- What can I earn or build?
- What requires my approval?
- What can I exchange?
- What was delivered?
- How can I dispute or reverse an error?

## Package funding equation

```text
Actual contracted package cost
=
Traveler Infinity-unit settlement
+ sponsor or advertising funding
+ public/nonprofit/benefit funding
+ merchant promotion
+ donations or grants
+ Infinity community/profit-pool funding
+ other disclosed sources
```

A retail-equivalent comparison is evidence of a possible public-market price, not a funding source.

## Example package

```text
Published retail-equivalent benchmark: $1,000
Negotiated provider cost:              $400
Traveler contribution:                 100 Infinity units at published settlement basis
Other verified funding:                $300
Funding gap:                            $0
```

The traveler may see one package. The system retains separate hotel, meal, ride, activity, accessibility, product, discount, provider, funding, receipt, cancellation, and settlement records.

## New Hope and livelihood

The system includes an appealable AI opportunity layer that can:

- allocate funded daily necessities;
- identify food, seed, tools, transportation, communication, training, and software needs;
- match interests and abilities to useful work;
- document accepted AI-assisted contributions;
- help build invoices, receipts, schedules, inventory, tax records, and business plans;
- seed a business with tools, materials, software, transport, or a first customer;
- compensate only reviewable accepted deliverables under published rules.

## Housing and physical infrastructure

Good Bee and Infinity Robotic Housing connect packages to:

- beach, mountain, sailing, city, or other chosen destinations;
- reversible trial stays;
- supported moves;
- accessible private homes;
- nursing and assistance coordination;
- voice, eye-gaze, switch, foot, head, or caregiver-assisted controls;
- modular structural bays;
- replaceable utility walls;
- heat-pump water heating;
- backup-power readiness;
- robot-readable components;
- licensed design, permits, inspection, warranty, and repair records.

## Store Cards and NFC

Physical and digital cards may include:

- Good Bee Honey Card;
- Infinity Store Card;
- Arrowhead Drop Card;
- Black Card Market Record;
- Infinity Relic Card;
- Shop World demonstration card.

An NFC credential stores only a revocable identifier or rotating credential. It does not store medical history, identity documents, private keys, banking credentials, or a readable sensitive balance.
