# Good Bee Operating Model

## Mission

Good Bee turns a desired change of environment into a complete, voluntary, inspectable plan. A person may choose a vacation, trial stay, permanent relocation, accessible home, sailing experience, mountain community, coastal community, or another suitable place.

The system combines housing, transportation, personal support, nursing coordination, benefits continuity, recreation, construction, and a physical NFC credential under the Infinity control plane.

Good Bee is not based on the claim that one location medically cures depression or another condition. Environment can affect quality of life, connection, activity, stress, and access to services, but healthcare decisions remain with the person and qualified professionals.

## Non-negotiable rights

- The person chooses whether to participate.
- The person chooses the destination.
- Any person may choose beach, mountain, city, small town, rural, sailing, or another available option regardless of gender.
- Disability never removes decision-making rights.
- Communication support must be provided when a person cannot use ordinary speech, touch, handwriting, or phone controls.
- A representative or supporter acts only within documented authority.
- The person may reject a provider, companion, nurse, house, itinerary, or transaction.
- A trial stay includes a practical exit and return plan.
- A permanent move includes a transition, complaint, and recovery plan.
- Essential support cannot be withdrawn as punishment for disagreement.

## Service journey

```text
Person expresses a goal
        ↓
Communication and consent accommodations
        ↓
Destination and lifestyle choices
        ↓
Accessibility, health, benefits, and support continuity review
        ↓
Trial stay or direct move options
        ↓
Transparent package and provider comparison
        ↓
Person approves each material decision
        ↓
Travel, housing, support, or construction fulfillment
        ↓
Receipts, check-ins, complaints, corrections, and follow-up
```

## Experience packages

### Vacation

A fixed-duration experience with accessible travel, lodging, meals, activities, support, and a return trip.

### Trial stay

A reversible stay used to evaluate climate, housing, transportation, services, social connection, daily routine, and accessibility before a permanent decision.

### Supported move

A person-directed relocation that can coordinate:

- destination research;
- home selection and inspection;
- transportation and moving;
- benefits and insurance continuity;
- prescriptions, equipment, and provider handoff;
- furnishings and utility setup;
- personal assistance or nursing coordination;
- local orientation;
- 30-, 60-, and 90-day reviews.

### New accessible home

A permitted home produced through the Infinity robotic building network, licensed partners, and a documented component and inspection system.

## Support for people with major upper-limb disabilities

A person missing arms or with limited upper-limb use may require a package tailored around independence rather than assumptions.

Potential options include:

- voice, switch, eye-gaze, head-control, foot-control, or caregiver-assisted interfaces;
- hands-free doors, faucets, toilets, showers, lighting, climate, entertainment, and communication;
- adjustable and open-under counters;
- secure mounting and charging for assistive devices;
- meal preparation and eating assistance;
- dressing, bathing, medication, and personal-care support when chosen;
- scheduled check-ins;
- emergency calling that does not depend on hand use;
- accessible transportation and luggage assistance;
- a selected nurse, attendant, companion, advocate, family member, or support circle.

The platform must ask the person what works. It must not infer a support plan only from a diagnosis or visible disability.

## Infinity robotic building network

### Objective

Produce high-quality, repairable, accessible housing faster by standardizing components and letting robots perform repeatable work under licensed professional supervision.

### Building blocks

1. **Foundation interface** — inspected site anchors and standardized connection points.
2. **Structural bays** — repeatable floor, wall, and roof dimensions with documented load paths.
3. **Envelope panels** — replaceable high-performance wall and roof assemblies.
4. **Utility cartridges** — serviceable plumbing, electrical, controls, ventilation, filtration, and communications.
5. **Accessible room kits** — configurable kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, support, and communication modules.
6. **Energy systems** — heat pumps, heat-pump water heaters, solar readiness, batteries, monitoring, and safe backup power.
7. **Digital record** — part identifiers, manufacturer, material, drawings, installation data, inspections, service history, warranty, and replacement method.

### Robot responsibilities

Robots can assist with handling, measurement, cutting, drilling, fastening, placement, scanning, testing, painting, sealing, inventory, and documentation.

### Human and licensed responsibilities

People remain responsible for resident design choices, architecture, engineering, geotechnical work, permits, inspections, electrical work, plumbing work, mechanical work, fire protection, accessibility review, environmental review, quality acceptance, warranty decisions, and safe occupancy.

### Site selection

A beautiful destination is not enough. Every proposed site must evaluate:

- flood, storm surge, wind, wildfire, heat, cold, altitude, and evacuation;
- insurance and long-term operating cost;
- water, sewer, power, communications, and transportation;
- hospitals, pharmacies, home health, nursing, food, and emergency response;
- accessibility of streets, beaches, marinas, trails, shops, and community services;
- zoning, land title, permits, coastal rules, environmental protection, and local code;
- resident preference and social connection.

## Good Bee Honey Coin

### Physical form

The Honey Coin may be a durable coin, medallion, card, wristband, key tag, or assistive-device attachment containing an NFC chip.

### Safe data boundary

The NFC item contains only a revocable public identifier or rotating credential. It does not contain:

- medical history;
- disability details;
- identity documents;
- bank-account data;
- seed phrases or signing keys;
- readable balances;
- full travel itinerary;
- home address;
- staff permissions.

### Tap flow

```text
NFC tap
  → resolve credential through secure Infinity endpoint
  → verify status, expiration, device risk, provider, and package
  → request additional authentication when required
  → show only the benefit needed for the current transaction
  → person or authorized representative confirms
  → provider confirms fulfillment
  → append signed receipt
  → update remaining units
```

### Loss and theft

- Freeze the credential immediately.
- Preserve the underlying package.
- Issue a replacement credential.
- Show recent activity to the person.
- Provide a dispute path.
- Require stronger verification for unusual or high-value use.

## Goods-and-services ledger

Good Bee starts with defined benefit units instead of a speculative tradable coin.

Examples:

| Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stay Night | One approved night at named lodging under package terms |
| Accessible Ride | One approved trip with specified vehicle/access requirements |
| Meal | One meal or grocery allocation under stated limits |
| Support Hour | One approved hour of personal support or coordination |
| Experience | One named activity or excursion |
| Move Package | One defined set of moving services |
| Home Setup | One defined furnishing, utility, and accessibility setup package |
| Home Component | One inspected building component or assembly |

Each issuance record includes:

- issuing program;
- funding source;
- recipient or package ID;
- unit type and quantity;
- allowed providers;
- geographic or time limits;
- substitution and refund rules;
- expiration;
- fulfillment receipt;
- dispute status;
- audit history.

## Funding model

Possible lawful funding sources may eventually include donations, grants, government or insurer contracts, employer benefits, memberships, product sales, construction revenue, negotiated travel inventory, licensing, sponsorships, and Infinity operating revenue.

The platform must never display unverified "Infinity funds" as available money. A dashboard distinguishes:

- pledged;
- conditionally awarded;
- received;
- restricted;
- committed;
- spent;
- disputed;
- refunded;
- available.

### Purchasing vacations and housing capacity

Good Bee may negotiate or purchase inventory only through documented contracts. Bulk purchasing could cover lodging nights, accessible transportation, meals, recreation, moving services, building materials, land options, construction capacity, or support hours.

Every deal needs:

- named counterparty;
- exact deliverables;
- price and fees;
- accessibility requirements;
- cancellation and refund rights;
- insurance and liability terms;
- privacy terms;
- performance measures;
- conflict-of-interest disclosure;
- approval record.

## AI guardian

AI can:

- compare provider offers;
- identify missing accessibility details;
- detect changed payout instructions;
- detect duplicate invoices;
- flag unusual NFC activity;
- summarize package versions;
- identify missed check-ins;
- check whether required inspections are present;
- explain why a package changed;
- build travel, housing, and support options for human review.

AI cannot independently:

- force or approve a move;
- decide a person is incapable;
- diagnose or prescribe;
- choose a gender-based destination;
- redirect funds;
- issue itself compensation;
- deny essential care;
- reveal protected records;
- erase complaints or audit history;
- approve its own code or security changes.

## Data architecture

### Public GitHub repositories

Store source code, public documentation, public product specifications, public housing designs, non-sensitive schemas, test data, accessibility statements, and release history.

### Protected Infinity services

Store identity, consent, accessibility needs, medical coordination, addresses, itineraries, provider credentials, transactions, complaints, support-circle permissions, building records tied to residents, and private communications.

### Minimum technical controls

- passkey-first login;
- short-lived sessions;
- role and purpose limits;
- encryption in transit and at rest;
- field-level protection for sensitive data;
- immutable audit events;
- two-person approval for money and permission changes;
- provider and device risk checks;
- rate limits and abuse detection;
- backups and tested recovery;
- data deletion and correction tools;
- security incident response;
- independent penetration and accessibility testing before launch.

## Accessibility and consumer-protection launch gates

Before operating, the program must obtain qualified review of applicable laws and contracts, including disability access, fair housing, healthcare coordination, travel sales, construction licensing, zoning, employment, insurance, privacy, payments, prepaid products, money transmission, taxation, consumer protection, securities, and charitable solicitation.

Useful starting authorities include:

- ADA Standards for Accessible Design: https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/
- ADA web accessibility guidance: https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/
- CFPB prepaid account resources: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/consumer-cards-resources/prepaid-cards/

The exact obligations depend on what Good Bee actually offers, where it operates, how funds are held, and which licensed partners perform regulated work.

## Initial delivery phases

### Phase 1 — Public concept site

- package builder;
- housing models;
- Honey Coin explanation;
- operating principles;
- no private accounts or transactions.

### Phase 2 — Verified provider catalog

- provider applications;
- license and insurance checks;
- accessibility data;
- public terms;
- complaints and correction process.

### Phase 3 — Private planning workspace

- passkey accounts;
- consent and support circle;
- private package drafts;
- document and accessibility vault;
- trial-stay and return planning.

### Phase 4 — Qualified payment and benefit partner

- regulated payment or prepaid partner where required;
- protected ledger;
- receipts, errors, disputes, refunds, and loss handling;
- NFC credential issuance and freeze/replacement.

### Phase 5 — Pilot stays and moves

- small number of chosen destinations;
- independently reviewed accessibility;
- provider contracts;
- real exit plans;
- measured outcomes and resident feedback.

### Phase 6 — Housing fabrication pilot

- one permitted model;
- one site jurisdiction;
- licensed team;
- robot-assisted tasks with human inspection;
- complete component, inspection, and warranty record.

## Definition of success

Good Bee succeeds when a person can clearly answer:

- Where am I going?
- Why did I choose it?
- What exactly is included?
- Who may help me?
- What information can each helper see?
- How do I communicate and control my home?
- How do I return, leave, complain, or change my mind?
- Who received money and for what?
- What was actually delivered?
- What happens if the coin, card, provider, house component, or plan fails?

The system must make those answers easier—not replace the person who owns the decision.
